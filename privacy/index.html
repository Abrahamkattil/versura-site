Privacy policy

Updated November 16, 2026

The short version: Versura has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data. Your verses and progress stay on your iPhone. A few features need the internet, and each one is explained below.

What stays on your iPhone

Your verses, progress, reviews, collections, badges and settings are stored on your iPhone. They are not sent to us, and we have no servers that hold them.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics companies in the app.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any information.
  • No account: we never ask for your name, email or password to use Versura.

Saying verses aloud

When you recite a verse aloud, Versura uses Apple's speech recognition to turn your voice into text so it can check your words. On iPhones that can do this themselves, your voice stays on the phone. On others, the audio is sent to Apple to be turned into text, under Apple's privacy policy. Versura never saves or sends us your recordings. This only happens if you allow microphone and speech recognition access, and you can turn them off at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

Bible translations

The Berean Standard Bible is built into the app and works without the internet. If you choose another translation, Versura asks that translation's licensed service for the text of each verse you use:

  • ESV from Crossway's ESV API
  • NIV from the YouVersion Platform (or API.Bible)
  • NLT and NKJV from API.Bible

These requests contain only the verse reference, such as “John 3:16”. Like any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address. Nothing that identifies you is sent, and the text is then kept on your iPhone. Each service has its own privacy policy. Not every translation may be available in every version of the app.

Learning with others (Together)

Together lets you practice alongside people you invite. It works through Apple's iCloud, so it needs you to be signed in to iCloud, and nothing is shared until you invite someone or accept an invite.

  • Within a group, the people in it can see the group's name, the name and avatar you choose, which days you practiced, which verses you've learned, and the tap-only notes you send. This is stored in iCloud by Apple, shared only with that group, and not visible to us.
  • Friend codes: so people can find you by your code, your chosen name, avatar and friend code are kept in a directory in Versura's iCloud storage. We can see this directory, but nothing else about you.

You can leave a group at any time. Versura has no typed messages between people.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. No reminder information is sent to us.

Emailing us

If you send feedback or report a problem, we receive your email address and what you write. If you leave “Include app details” on, the email also includes your app version, iOS version and iPhone model. We use this only to reply and to fix problems, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

Versura is meant for everyone, including families. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Together relies on Apple's iCloud accounts and follows Apple's rules for children's accounts.

Deleting your information

  • To remove everything Versura keeps on your iPhone, go to You › Settings › Start over, or delete the app.
  • To stop sharing with a group, leave it in Together.
  • To have your friend code or any email you sent us deleted, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and tell you in the app before the change matters to you.
