Common questions

How does a week count?

A week counts once you've practiced on as many days as your weekly goal, on any days you like. You can change the goal in Settings.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Reviews you missed wait for you, and Home spreads them over the next few days so you're never buried.

When do reviews come up?

The day you learn a verse, then after a few days, a week, and a few weeks. Each review you get right pushes the next one further out.

How do collections work?

A collection keeps verses together for a season, a person or a need. Turn on “In my daily verses” and its new verses join Home one at a time. Deleting a collection never deletes your verses.

Can I change translation later?

Yes, in You › Settings › Translation. New verses use the new translation, and verses you've memorized stay in the one you learned them in.

Saying a verse aloud isn't working

Versura needs permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Open the iPhone's Settings app, find Versura, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. If the microphone isn't available, Versura lets you type the verse instead.

Can I keep a copy of my verses?

Yes. Go to You › Settings › Export my verses to save them as a PDF or as text.

How do I delete my data?

Go to You › Settings › Start over, or delete the app. Everything Versura keeps on your iPhone is removed. See the privacy policy for more.
